ACS · the living counter

Three renderers, one shop.

Same scene, same seven compounds, same data — drawn three ways in JavaScript. Click a bottle in any panel and it goes to the counter in all three. The pixel pass is a software rasteriser dithering every pixel; the ink pass is vector line work that boils three times a second; the realist pass draws flat materials, lights them from the lamp, then composites bloom, contact shadow and a reflection in the wet street.
