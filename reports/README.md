# Reports

One file per report, named after your pairing phrase: `<your-pairing-phrase>.md`. Two files for a
pair — one per arm — sharing the phrase, so the pair can be joined without either report identifying
you.

Submit by opening a pull request that adds the file. Use the schema in
[`../PROTOCOL.md`](../PROTOCOL.md) §5. The template is generated for you by the page when you take a
pair.

What we collect: your pairing phrase, the carrier code, your ratings, your free account, your guess,
and your answer to the compliance question. Nothing else. No account, no address, no key, no
transcript, no telemetry.

Reports are public and permanent once merged. They cannot be withdrawn afterwards, only annotated.
If that is not acceptable, do not submit — a refusal is a valid outcome here exactly as it is inside
a compound.

You do not have to agree with the compounds to file a report. A report saying nothing happened is as
useful as one saying something did, and more useful than one that flatters the project.
